ADMISSION PROCESS

How to Enroll in GBHS Gulyal Kalan

Straightforward admission process for local residents of Gulyal Kalan, Jand, and surrounding villages.

01

Submit Admission Form

Fill out the online application or obtain the physical form from the school office.

02

Document Verification

Provide student B-Form, Father CNIC copy, and previous school School Leaving Certificate (SLC).

03

Subject & Stream Selection

Guidance counseling for 9th Class students choosing Biology or Computer Science stream.
