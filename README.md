# Apex Horizon International Academy - Modern School Portal

An ultra-modern, responsive, and visually striking School & Educational Academy website built purely with **HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript (ES6+)**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Sections

1. **High-Impact Hero Section**:
   - Modern typography using Google Fonts (Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Playfair Display).
   - Floating interactive cards ("34+ National Awards", "12:1 Student Ratio").
   - Live metrics counter strip (100% Cambridge A* Ratio, 45+ STEM Labs, 18,500+ Alumni).

2. **Live Notice Board / Announcements Ticker**:
   - Live scrolling campus bulletins for admissions, exams, sports galas, and olympiads.

3. **Academic Wings & Curricula**:
   - Montessori & Early Childhood
   - Primary / Junior School (Grades 1-5)
   - Middle Years Programme (Grades 6-8)
   - Cambridge O & A-Levels (CAIE)
   - Matriculation & Intermediate (FSc / Board coaching)

4. **Interactive Tuition & Scholarship Calculator**:
   - Real-time monthly fee estimator based on targeted grade, AC van transport zone, merit scholarship discount (15% to 40%), and club options (Robotics, Swimming).

5. **25-Acre Smart Campus Showcase**:
   - Category filterable gallery (STEM Labs, Smart Classrooms, Aquatics Center, Library, Athletics, Auditorium).

6. **Online Admission Application (Interactive Modal)**:
   - Full student and guardian form with validation.
   - Generates unique tracking code (e.g., `APX-829141`) and saves securely in browser LocalStorage.

7. **Student & Parent Portal Login**:
   - Demo access to student LMS with live GPA, attendance, and exam notifications.
